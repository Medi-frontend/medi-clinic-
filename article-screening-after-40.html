Preventive Care

The screening schedule you should not skip after 40

After 40, preventive visits become less about doing more tests and more about choosing the right checks at the right time.

Core checks to discuss

Your doctor may recommend blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes screening, kidney function, eye checks, dental care, and mental health screening based on your history.

Screenings can be personal

  • Cervical, breast, prostate, and colorectal cancer screening depend on age, sex, family history, and previous results.
  • Bone health checks may matter earlier for people at higher risk.
  • Vaccines, medication reviews, and lifestyle risks should be reviewed yearly.

Bring useful information

Bring your ID, medical aid details, medication list, previous results, and any family history of heart disease, diabetes, stroke, or cancer.

Plan your screening

We can help you choose the right checks for your age, symptoms, and family history.
