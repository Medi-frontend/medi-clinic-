Pediatrics

Building a vaccination schedule your child will keep

A good vaccination plan is clear, calm, and easy to follow. The goal is protection without making clinic visits feel frightening.

Keep one record

Store your child’s immunisation card safely and bring it to every visit. A single record helps the nurse or doctor see what is due and what has already been given.

Make visits calmer

  • Explain the visit in simple words before you arrive.
  • Bring a comfort item, snack, or quiet activity for waiting.
  • Ask which vaccines are due today and what mild reactions to expect.
  • Book the next visit before leaving so the schedule stays on track.

When to call first

Call the clinic if your child has a high fever, a previous serious vaccine reaction, or if you are unsure whether a missed vaccine can be caught up safely.
