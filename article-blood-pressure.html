Heart Health

5 habits that lower blood pressure naturally

Blood pressure improves most reliably when small routines become easy enough to repeat. These habits support your heart between clinic visits.

Start with measurement

Knowing your numbers is the first step. Take readings at similar times of day, sit quietly first, and bring the pattern to your doctor instead of relying on one reading.

Build the daily basics

  • Walk briskly for 20 to 30 minutes on most days.
  • Choose more vegetables, beans, fruit, oats, fish, and unsalted nuts.
  • Reduce salty packaged foods and check labels for sodium.
  • Limit alcohol and avoid smoking or vaping.
  • Protect sleep by keeping a regular bedtime and reducing late caffeine.

Know when to ask for help

Book a checkup if readings stay high, if you have headaches, chest discomfort, shortness of breath, dizziness, or if you are already on medication and your numbers change.
